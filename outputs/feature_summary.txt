MATHEMATICAL CONCEPT & RELEVANCE TO INFORMATION RETRIEVAL:
TF-IDF (Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency) is a foundational weighting
scheme in Information Retrieval. It transforms variable-length unstructured text
into fixed-dimensional numerical vectors in the Vector Space Model (VSM).

1. Term Frequency (TF):
   Quantifies how frequently a term occurs inside a single SMS message.
   With sublinear TF scaling (1 + log(tf)), diminishing returns are applied so
   that an SMS containing 10 repetitions of 'free' does not carry 10 times the weight
   of a single occurrence.

2. Inverse Document Frequency (IDF):
   IDF = log((1 + N) / (1 + df)) + 1, where N is the total number of documents
   and df is the document frequency of the term.
   Terms ubiquitous across almost all messages (e.g., 'call', 'today', 'just') receive
   low IDF scores, while discriminative terms concentrated primarily in spam
   (e.g., 'claim', 'urgent', 'winner', 'txt') receive high IDF scores.

3. L2 Normalization:
   Each document vector is normalized to unit length Euclidean norm (||v|| = 1),
   mitigating bias caused by variations in message length.
