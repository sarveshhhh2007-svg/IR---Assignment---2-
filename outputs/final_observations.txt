6. PRACTICAL TRADE-OFF ANALYSIS IN INFORMATION RETRIEVAL
   - The Danger of Pure Accuracy:
     Because legitimate messages constitute roughly 87% of the dataset, a trivial
     dummy classifier predicting 'ham' for all inputs would achieve ~87% accuracy
     while catching exactly 0% of spam. Thus, Accuracy alone is deceptive.

   - Cost Asymmetry (False Positives vs. False Negatives):
     * False Positives (legitimate SMS flagged as spam): Highly destructive. Missing
       a bank one-time password (OTP), medical alert, or critical personal message
       destroys user trust.
     * False Negatives (spam entering inbox): Annoying, but far less damaging than
       losing critical legitimate communication.
     Therefore, an optimal spam filter requires very high Precision (near zero FP)
     while maximizing Recall (minimizing FN), best evaluated by Spam F1-Score.

   - Algorithmic Suitability on Sparse TF-IDF Vector Spaces:
     * Support Vector Machine (LinearSVC): Outstanding performer. High-dimensional
       sparse text representations are linearly separable. SVM finds the optimal
       maximum-margin hyperplanes without suffering from the curse of dimensionality.
     * Multinomial Naive Bayes: Extremely fast, lightweight, and mathematically
       well-suited for word frequency representations. Yields high precision.
     * Decision Tree: Prone to capturing noise on large vocabularies unless constrained;
       orthogonal axis splits struggle with complex phrase combinations.
     * KNN: Computationally demanding at test time (O(N) distance checks across all
       training vectors) and sensitive to distance metric choice in sparse spaces.

7. FINAL EVIDENCE-BASED RECOMMENDATION
   Based on the rigorous empirical benchmark across identical stratified test data,
   'Support Vector Machine' is strongly recommended as the best classifier for SMS Spam
   Detection. It delivers an outstanding Spam F1-Score of 0.9323
   (Accuracy: 0.9835, Precision: 0.9512,
   Recall: 0.9141), effectively suppressing false positives while
   retaining robust detection of evasive spam messages with ultra-fast inference.
